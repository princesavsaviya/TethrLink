# Privacy Policy for TethrLink

**Last updated:** June 24, 2026

TethrLink is an open-source screen-streaming tool that connects a Linux desktop client to an Android device over a local USB or network connection.

## Data Collection

TethrLink does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not use analytics, crash reporting, or any third-party SDKs.

## Data Transmission

All video and control data transmitted by TethrLink stays strictly between your Linux device and your Android device over a direct, local connection (USB tethering or local network). No data is sent to any external server, and no data leaves your local network at any point.

## Permissions

TethrLink requests only the network and USB permissions required to establish a direct connection between your devices. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or any other device data.

## Third-Party Services

TethrLink does not integrate with or share data with any third-party service.

## Children's Privacy

TethrLink does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
